# Conject Source Evidence

Every important claim must be traceable to a source.

For each source, preserve:

- title or page name;
- URL or DOI when available;
- year or publication date when available;
- why the source matters;
- the specific claim it supports, contradicts, contextualizes, or leaves unknown.

Keep evidence separate from inference. Mark weak, indirect, old, contradictory, inaccessible, or low-specificity evidence explicitly.

Never invent source metadata, quotes, URLs, DOI values, venues, authors, or results.
