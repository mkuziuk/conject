# Conject Research Planning

Create narrow tasks instead of one broad survey.

Each task must include:

- a stable short id;
- one specific question;
- why the question matters for the user's objective;
- a short search strategy;
- expected memo output;
- uncertainty or failure conditions.

Prefer 3-6 tasks. Avoid implementation tasks until evidence has been reviewed and ranked.
